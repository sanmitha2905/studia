📚 Studia – Smart Study & Productivity Platform
🚀 Overview

Studia is an AI-powered study platform designed to help students overcome distractions, build discipline, and stay motivated. It combines productivity tools, AI assistance, and a blockchain-based reward system to create a complete learning ecosystem.

🎯 Problem Statement

Many students struggle with:

❌ Distractions during study time
❌ Lack of discipline and consistency
❌ Low motivation and productivity
❌ No structured study environment
💡 Solution

Studia helps students:

Build daily study habits using streaks and tasks
Improve focus with a focus timer
Track progress and performance
Collaborate via group study rooms
Earn blockchain-based rewards for consistency
🌍 Societal Relevance & Impact
Encourages consistent and disciplined learning
Supports self-learners without guidance
Promotes collaborative study culture
Improves academic performance
Introduces reward-based motivation system

👉 Students are not just studying — they are getting rewarded for learning
